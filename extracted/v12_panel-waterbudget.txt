    Precipitation Character Transition
    The 1980 snow fraction baseline of 80.4% is declining at −0.18%/yr (FigD, corrected from prior 84% anchor). Below ~65–70% snow fraction, the dominant recharge mechanism shifts from sustained snowmelt hydrology to episodic pulse events with lower reservoir capture efficiency. This threshold has infrastructure implications: conveyance systems and reservoir operating rules designed for snowmelt become suboptimal for pulse-dominated hydrology. At the current rate, the threshold is reached approximately 2053–2058 absent acceleration. Policy interventions must address both volume delivered AND capture mode simultaneously.
